Process: Escalate major incident
Key: incident_escalation
Owner: Herr Weber
Trigger: Monitoring alert or user-reported outage

1. [Service Desk] Log incident | system=Jira Service Management; r=Service Desk Agent
2. [Service Desk] Triage
3. [Service Desk] Assign incident to resolver group | r=Service Desk Agent; a=Service Desk Lead
? Major incident?
-> Yes: Convene major incident bridge
-> No: Resolve incident in resolver group
4. [Major Incident Manager] Convene major incident bridge | r=Major Incident Manager
5. [Resolver Group] Resolve incident in resolver group | r=Resolver Group Engineer; a=Resolver Group Lead, Service Owner
6. [Service Desk] Notification
