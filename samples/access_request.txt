Process: Fulfil application access request
Key: access_request
Version: 0.9
Owner: IT Service Desk Lead
Purpose: Grant, review and record user access to business applications in line with least-privilege principles.
Scope: Covers access to all internally managed business applications. Privileged administrator access follows the separate privileged access procedure.
Trigger: Access request raised in the service portal
Outcome: Access granted and recorded

1. [Employee] Raise access request | system=Service Portal; r=Employee; a=Employee; i=Line Manager
2. [Line Manager] Confirm business need for access | control=Line manager authorisation; system=Service Portal; r=Line Manager; a=Line Manager; c=Employee; i=Service Desk
? Does the role require elevated rights? | control=Segregation of duties check
-> Elevated rights required: Obtain application owner authorisation
-> Standard rights sufficient: Provision access in the target system
3. [Application Owner] Obtain application owner authorisation | control=Four-eyes approval; system=Service Portal; r=Application Owner; a=Application Owner; c=Information Security; i=Line Manager
4. [Service Desk] Provision access in the target system | system=Active Directory; r=Service Desk Agent; a=Service Desk Lead; i=Employee
5. [Service Desk] Record access grant in the access register | control=Access recertification evidence; system=Access Register; r=Service Desk Agent; a=Service Desk Lead; c=Information Security; i=Line Manager
6. [Employee] Confirm access is working | system=Service Portal; r=Employee; a=Service Desk Lead
